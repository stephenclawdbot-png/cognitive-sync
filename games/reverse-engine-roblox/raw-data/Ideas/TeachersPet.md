# 👩‍🏫 Teacher's Pet

> **Formula:** A (Timer-Reveal, like Knockout!)
> **Theme:** School/Teacher trend
> **Status:** Concept - needs deeper exploration

---

## Core Concept

```
Everyone is a STUDENT in a classroom.
Each student has a SECRET TARGET to throw paper at.
ONE student is secretly "TEACHER'S PET" (the snitch).
Teacher's Pet reports someone each round.
You don't know who the snitch is...
```

---

## The Mechanics

### Secret Target (From Knockout!)
- Each round, you're assigned ONE classmate to hit
- Throw paper ball at them
- They don't know you're targeting them

### The Snitch Twist
- ONE random student is secretly "Teacher's Pet"
- Teacher's Pet picks someone to REPORT each round
- Reported player gets extra penalty
- Nobody knows who the snitch is
- Snitch changes each round? Or stays same?

### Resolution
- Paper hits = knockback toward "detention" (edge)
- Reported player = extra knockback? Or other penalty?
- Fall into detention zone = eliminated

|---------|-------------------|

| Shrinking arena | ✅ "Classroom gets smaller" |
| Timer pressure | ✅ "Teacher returning in X" |
