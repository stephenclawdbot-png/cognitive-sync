# Copycat Chaos 🐱😺😺

**Status:** SAVED - High potential (unique concept)
**Theme:** Cats blending in with NPCs
**Style:** Real-time deception + social deduction

---

## 🎮 Core Concept

Arena filled with NPC cats walking around. All PLAYERS also look identical to NPCs. One player is the HUNTER who must identify REAL players among the fakes. Hidden through BEHAVIOR, not invisibility.

**The Twist:** You're not invisible — you're DISGUISED. Survival = acting like an NPC.

---

├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                 │

## 🤖 NPC Behavior Patterns

Players must LEARN and MIMIC these:

| Pattern | Description | How NPCs Do It |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **Patrol** | Walk in straight lines | Turn at walls, consistent speed |
| **Wander** | Random movement | Pause occasionally, change direction |
| **Gather** | Group up in areas | Move toward food bowls, sunny spots |
| **Groom** | Stop and lick paws | Stand still for 2-3 seconds |
| **Nap** | Lay down | Only in certain "nap zones" |

### Tells That Reveal Players
```
❌ Running (NPCs never run)
❌ Jumping (NPCs don't jump)
❌ Stopping in wrong spots (not nap zones)
❌ Moving against patrol paths
❌ Sudden direction changes
❌ Following other players
❌ Looking at hunter (staring)
```

## 🎯 Hunter Mechanics

### Pounce Attack
- Click on a cat to POUNCE
- Short dash + attack
- Cooldown: 2 seconds

### Correct Pounce (Hit a Player)
- Player is ELIMINATED
- Player's disguise breaks (revealed as player briefly)
- Hunter continues hunting

### Wrong Pounce (Hit an NPC)
- NPC hisses and runs away
- Hunter loses 5 seconds from timer
- Creates chaos (NPCs scatter temporarily)
- 3 wrong pounces = Hunter is eliminated!

### Hunter Strategy
- Watch for unnatural movement
- Look for players that react to you
- Cause chaos (pounce NPCs) to see who panics
- But don't waste all 3 mistakes!

---

## 🐱 Player Survival Strategies

### Beginner
- Follow an NPC closely
- Copy their exact movements
- Don't panic when hunter is near

### Intermediate
- Learn patrol routes
- Know safe "nap zones"
- Mix behaviors (patrol, groom, wander)

### Advanced
- Bait hunter into wrong pounces
- Use NPC scatter chaos to reposition
- Predict hunter's search pattern
- Stay in groups of NPCs for cover

## 🗺️ Map Design Ideas

### "Cat Café"
- Indoor café environment
- Tables, chairs (hiding spots)
- Nap zones on cushions
- Food bowls (gather points)
- Sunny window spots

### "Backyard"
- Outdoor garden
- Bushes (partial cover)
- Fish pond (NPCs gather here)
- Fence patrol routes
- Shed for hiding

### "Cat Tower"
- Vertical map
- Multiple platforms
- NPCs climb up/down
- Risk: jumping to platforms (sus behavior)

## 📊 Scoring System

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Survive full round | +100 |
| Survive 15+ seconds | +50 |
| Hunter: Find a player | +75 |
| Hunter: Find ALL players | +200 bonus |
| Cause hunter to wrong pounce | +25 |

### Win Condition
- Play 5-7 rounds
- Everyone rotates as hunter
- Most points at end = WINNER
