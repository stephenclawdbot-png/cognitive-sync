# 👑 Capture The Crown

> **Tagline:** Wear the crown, become the target!

---

## Core Loop
- Crown spawns in center
- Grab crown = KING (earn points per second)
- Others attack king to steal
- Hold longest = wins

---

## Mechanics

### The Crown
| State | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Unclaimed | Spawns center, anyone can grab |
| Claimed | Holder = King, earns 1 point/sec |
| Dropped | Brief cooldown before pickup |

### Being King
- **Visible** - Crown glows, everyone sees you
- **Target** - All players hunt you
- **Tactical drop** - Can drop crown on purpose (bait?)

### Combat
- Simple attack to steal (punch? bump?)
- King has no combat advantage
- Or... King gets a weapon? (risk/reward)

---

## Why It Works
- ✅ Simple objective (hold crown = win)
- ✅ Constant action (king always hunted)
- ✅ Natural drama (clutch steals)
- ✅ No camping (king is visible)
- ✅ Comeback potential (steal at last second)

---

## Visual Notes
- Arena (castle? colosseum? playground?)
- Crown = big, glowing, obvious
- King spotlight/aura
- Point counter visible to all
- Steal effect = dramatic
