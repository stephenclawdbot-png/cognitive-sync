# 🪝 Grapple Wars

> **Tagline:** Hook, swing, YEET - Master the grapple!

---

## Core Loop
- Everyone has grappling hook
- Grapple to surfaces to move
- Grapple ENEMIES to pull them
- Platform over void
- Pull enemies off = eliminated

---

## Mechanics

### Movement
| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Grapple wall/ceiling | Swing (momentum-based) |
| Grapple floor | Pull yourself (dash) |
| Release timing | Controls arc/speed |

### Combat
| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Grapple enemy | Pull them toward you |
| Both grapple each other | TUG OF WAR |
| Grapple + swing | Yeet enemy off platform |

### Physics
- **Momentum matters** - Speed from swings carries over
- **Mass matters?** - Heavier = harder to pull
- **Tug of war** - Mash or timing-based?

---

## Why It Works
- ✅ Movement skill expression (high ceiling)
- ✅ Unique mechanic (grapple PvP rare)
- ✅ Satisfying yeets (pulling enemies to death)
- ✅ Spectator friendly (acrobatic plays)
- ✅ Simple to learn, hard to master
