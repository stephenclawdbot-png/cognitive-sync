# 🪂 Parachute Panic

> **Tagline:** Vertical battle royale - Last one floating wins!

---

## Core Loop
- Everyone falling from sky
- All have parachutes
- Can KICK others to make them fall faster
- First to hit ground = eliminated
- Last floating = wins

---

## Mechanics

### Movement
| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Pull chute | Slow fall (safe mode) |
| Fold chute | Fast fall + kick ability |

**Trade-off:** Speed for offense. Fold to attack, but you're falling faster.

### Combat
- **Kick** - Only available when chute folded
- Kick connects = enemy drops faster
- Chain kicks = combo elimination
- Can't kick while parachute is open

### Bonus
- **Rings** - Fall through rings for bonus points
- Risk/reward: Rings might be in dangerous positions

---

## Why It Works
- ✅ Unique vertical perspective (not common in Roblox)
- ✅ Simple mechanic (open/close chute)
- ✅ Clear risk/reward (speed vs safety)
- ✅ Natural elimination (ground = death)
- ✅ Spectator friendly (watch from above)
