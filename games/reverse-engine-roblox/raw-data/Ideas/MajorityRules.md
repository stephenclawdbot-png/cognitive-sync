# ⬅️➡️ MAJORITY RULES

> **Formula:** A (Timer-Reveal)
> **Core Mechanic:** Pick a side, majority survives
> **Theme:** Cute animals choosing sides
> **Status:** ORIGINAL CONCEPT - Maximum Simplicity

---

## 🎯 Why This Is GENIUS

**The simplest possible game:**
- Two choices: LEFT or RIGHT
- That's it.
- Majority wins.

**But the mind games are INFINITE.**

## 🧠 The Mind Games (Why This WORKS)

### Round 1 (10 players):
```
"50/50 choice..."
"Most people go right... right?"
"Or do they think that and go left?"
"I'll just pick randomly..."
"NO WAIT, I should think about it—"
*Timer ends*
```

### Round 4 (5 players):
```
"3 vs 2 split will happen..."
"If I pick wrong, I'm OUT..."
"That guy ALWAYS picks left..."
"She switched last round..."
"WHAT DO I DO?!"
```

### Final 2 (Showdown):
```
"It's just us two..."
"Same side = TIE, we both survive"
"Different sides = ONE DIES"
"Do I match him or counter him?"
"He's looking left... is that a fake?"
"I'M GOING LEFT... NO RIGHT... NO—"
```

### The Infinite Loop:
```
"I think they'll go left..."
"But they know I think that..."
"So they'll go right..."
"But they know I know that..."
"So they'll go left..."
"BUT—"
*Brain explodes*
```

## 🔢 The Math

```
10 PLAYERS:
- 6-4 split: 6 survive, 4 die
- 7-3 split: 7 survive, 3 die
- 5-5 tie: Both survive OR random elimination

6 PLAYERS:
- 4-2: 4 survive
- 3-3 tie: Both survive

3 PLAYERS:
- 2-1: 2 survive, 1 dies
- THIS IS WHERE IT GETS INTENSE

2 PLAYERS (FINAL):
- Same side: Both survive, sudden death round
- Different: One dies, WINNER!
```
