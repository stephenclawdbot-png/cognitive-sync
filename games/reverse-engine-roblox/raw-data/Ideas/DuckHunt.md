# 🦆 Duck Hunt

> **Tagline:** Fly or die - Asymmetric survival!

---

## Core Loop
- One player (or few) is DUCK
- Others are HUNTERS with guns
- Duck flies, dodges, hides
- Hunters have limited ammo
- Duck survives X time = Duck wins
- Hunters hit duck = Hunters win

---

## Mechanics

### Duck Role
| Ability | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Flight | Full 3D movement, fast |
| Small hitbox | Hard to hit |
| Power-ups | Scattered around map |

### Hunter Role
| Ability | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Gun | Ranged attack |
| Limited ammo | Forces accuracy (10 shots?) |
| Slow movement | Can't chase effectively |
| Ammo pickups | Scattered, limited |

### Asymmetry
- Duck: Fast, fragile, evasive
- Hunters: Slow, powerful, limited resources

---

## Why It Works
- ✅ Classic concept (nostalgia)
- ✅ Asymmetric tension (different experiences)
- ✅ Role variety (play both sides)
- ✅ Skill expression (both roles)
- ✅ Quick rounds (30-60 sec?)

## Visual Notes
- Forest/hunting ground aesthetic
- Duck = cartoony, visible but small
- Hunters = orange vests, shotguns
- Cover objects (trees, bushes, rocks)
- Ammo crates glow

---

## Duck Power-ups
- **Speed Boost** - Temporary zoom
- **Decoy** - Fake duck appears
- **Invisibility** - Brief stealth
- **Shield** - Survive one hit

---

## Mode Variants

### Classic (1 Duck)
- 1 duck vs 4-8 hunters
- High tension
- Duck = skilled player

### Flock (Multiple Ducks)
- 3-4 ducks vs 8-12 hunters
- Hunters need to kill ALL ducks
- Ducks can sacrifice for others

### Infection
- Start with 1 duck
- Killed hunters become ducks
- Last hunter standing wins
