# 🐱 Cat Burglar

> **Formula:** B (Action-Reveal, like Silent Strike)
> **Theme:** Cats trend
> **Status:** Concept - needs deeper exploration

---

## Core Concept

```
Everyone is an INVISIBLE CAT.
Stealing CHEESE scattered around a room.
MOVING = you leave PAW PRINTS (briefly visible).
GRABBING CHEESE = fully REVEALS you.
Others can POUNCE on revealed cats.
Shrinking room (owner coming home!).
```

---

## The Mechanics

### Invisibility (From Silent Strike)
- All cats invisible by default
- Can't see other cats

### Movement = Reveal (The Twist)
- Moving leaves temporary paw prints
- Others can track your prints
- Stay still = fully hidden
- Move = partially revealed

### Grabbing = Full Reveal
- Cheese scattered around room
- Grabbing cheese = you become FULLY VISIBLE
- Vulnerable moment!
- But cheese = points

### Pounce Attack
- Can pounce on visible cats
- Successful pounce = they're eliminated
- Miss = YOU'RE revealed (risky!)

### Shrinking Room
- Owner coming home (timer)
- Safe area shrinks
- Forces cats closer together
- Forces grabbing cheese faster

## The Risk/Reward Loop

```
STAY STILL:
+ Fully invisible
- No points (no cheese)
- Zone shrinking toward you

MOVE:
+ Can get to cheese
- Leave paw prints (trackable)

GRAB CHEESE:
+ Get points!
- Fully visible for moment
- Might get pounced

POUNCE:
+ Can eliminate someone
- Miss = YOU'RE revealed
```
