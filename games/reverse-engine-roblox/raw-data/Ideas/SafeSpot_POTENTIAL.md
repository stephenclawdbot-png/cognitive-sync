# 🛡️ SAFE SPOT

> **Formula:** A (Timer-Reveal)
> **Core Mechanic:** Pick a platform, crowd = safety
> **Theme:** Cute animals scrambling for safety
> **Status:** ORIGINAL CONCEPT - High Potential

---

## 🎯 Why This Is ORIGINAL

Inverts the usual logic:
- Most games: Avoid the crowd, find your own space
- Safe Spot: JOIN the crowd or die

**The core tension:** Everyone wants to be where everyone else is... but where IS that?

│   PHASE 3: LOCK                                                 │

## 🧠 The Mind Games (Why This Is GENIUS)

### Basic Logic:
```
"I should go where most people go..."
"But I don't KNOW where most people will go..."
"Platform A looks obvious... so everyone picks A?"
"But if everyone THINKS A is obvious, maybe they pick B?"
"WHICH ONE IS THE CROWD GOING TO?!"
```

### Reading the Room:
```
"That guy is staring at Platform C..."
"Is he going there? Or is he faking?"
"Two people are near Platform A..."
"Are they going there or trying to bait me?"
"I'LL JUST FOLLOW THE MAJORITY... BUT WHERE?!"
```

### Late Game (2-3 platforms):
```
"Only 2 platforms left..."
"50/50 chance..."
"Or is it? Can I read their choices?"
"He went left last time..."
"She always follows the crowd..."
"I'll go RIGHT... no LEFT... no..."
```

### The Panic:
```
REVEAL happens →
"Wait, I'm the only one on this platform!"
"NO NO NO NO—"
*Platform crumbles*
*Falls into void*
"AAAAHHHH"
```

## 🔢 The Math

```
10 PLAYERS, 4 PLATFORMS:

Distribution examples:
• 5-3-1-1 → Platform with 5 = SAFE, others fall
• 4-4-1-1 → TIE: Both with 4 = SAFE
• 3-3-2-2 → TIE: Both with 3 = SAFE
• 4-3-2-1 → Only platform with 4 = SAFE

The goal: Be on the MOST crowded platform

TIE RULES:
If multiple platforms tie for most = ALL tied platforms safe
(Encourages spreading out a little, but not too much)
```
