# 🐕 Dog Park Dodge

> **Formula:** HYBRID (Knockout! + Silent Strike)
> **Theme:** Dogs trend
> **Status:** Concept - needs deeper exploration

---

## Core Concept

```
All DOGS have a TENNIS BALL.
Throw ball at others = KNOCKBACK (like Knockout!).
BUT: You're INVISIBLE when SITTING.
MOVE or THROW = REVEALS you (like Silent Strike).
Shrinking dog park.
```

---

## The Mechanics

### From Knockout!
- Everyone has a projectile (tennis ball)
- Hit someone = knockback toward edge
- Edge of park = eliminated
- Shrinking arena

### From Silent Strike
- Invisible when sitting still
- Moving or attacking reveals you
- Risk/reward for action

### The Hybrid
- Sitting dog = invisible
- Moving dog = visible
- Throwing = visible AND reveals your position
- Creates "ambush" gameplay

---

## The Loop

```
SIT STILL:
+ Invisible
+ Safe from being targeted
- Can't attack
- Can't dodge if someone throws at your spot

MOVE:
+ Can reposition
+ Can dodge
- Visible to everyone
- Can be targeted

THROW:
+ Can knock others out
+ Can score points
- Reveals you
- Lose your ball temporarily (fetch it back?)
```
