# ❄️ Snowball

> **Tagline:** Freeze or be frozen - Winter warfare!

---

## Core Loop
- Everyone throws snowballs
- Get hit = frozen briefly
- 3 hits = eliminated (turn into snowman)
- Last unfrozen = wins

---

## Mechanics

### Combat
| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Throw | Ranged attack, uses ammo |
| Pick up snow | Reload (from snow piles) |
| Dodge/duck | Avoid incoming |

### Defense
- **Snow walls** - Build temporary cover
- Walls block snowballs
- Walls melt over time
- Limited builds per player?

### Freeze System
- Hit 1: Slow for 2 sec
- Hit 2: Slower for 3 sec
- Hit 3: SNOWMAN (eliminated)
- Timer between hits? (prevents instant 3-tap)

---

## Why It Works
- ✅ Winter theme (seasonal appeal)
- ✅ Simple combat (throw, dodge, reload)
- ✅ Building adds depth (but stays simple)
- ✅ Visual feedback (frozen = obvious)
- ✅ Non-violent (snowballs = family friendly)
